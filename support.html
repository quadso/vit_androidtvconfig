Support

How to get help, and answers to the things that come up most.

Contact: quadso@quadso.com

Telling us the device (television or telephone), the programme, and what you expected to happen is usually enough to identify the problem.

A programme will not play

The programmes are streamed from Kringvarp Føroya's servers. If one will not start, first try another programme. If nothing plays, the problem is usually the network connection or KVF's service rather than the app.

Some programmes are restricted to viewers in the Faroe Islands. If you are elsewhere, playback may not start even though the programme is listed.

A new programme is not showing

The list of programmes is fetched when the app starts. Close the app fully and open it again. A newly added programme can take a few minutes to reach every device.

It does not continue where I stopped

The app offers to continue a programme you were part way through, and shows the position it would resume from. It does not offer this when you were less than ten seconds in, or when you had almost reached the end, because resuming there is more surprising than helpful.

Positions are kept on the device you were watching on. They do not follow you to another television or telephone.

What plays next

When a programme finishes, the next one in the series starts on its own. On television a Næsti partur button appears shortly before the end if you would rather not wait. For radio there is no such button, so that the closing moments are never cut short.

Reporting something wrong in the programme list

Titles, images and the order of episodes are taken from kvf.fo. If something is wrong or missing, say which programme and what you expected, and it can be corrected.

VIT is an independent app presenting programmes from Kringvarp Føroya.

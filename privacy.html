Privacy policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

The app collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours for it to be sent to.

What is stored, and where

Three things are kept on your device only:

  • how far into a programme you had watched, so it can offer to continue where you stopped;
  • which programme you last opened in each series, so it knows which one to offer;
  • a copy of the programme list, so the app still shows something when the network is unavailable.

None of this leaves your device, and none of it is readable by us or by anyone else. Removing the app removes all of it. There is nothing to request, export or delete elsewhere, because nothing is held elsewhere.

What happens when you use the app

The app makes ordinary internet requests to three places:

  • raw.githubusercontent.com — to fetch the list of programmes;
  • kvf.fo — to load programme images;
  • vod.kringvarp.fo — to stream the audio and video, which is served by Kringvarp Føroya.

As with visiting any website, those servers receive your device's IP address and the usual technical details of the request. That is a normal part of how the internet works and is not something the app adds. We neither receive nor store any of it. What those organisations do with their own server records is governed by their policies, not by this one.

Programmes and rights

The programmes are made and hosted by Kringvarp Føroya. The app plays them directly from KVF's own servers. It does not copy, download or store programmes on your device.

Children

Much of what the app offers is children's programming. The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included, and shows no advertising. There is nothing in it that asks a child for information, and nothing that could identify one.

Permissions

The app requests a single Android permission, internet access, without which it could not play anything. It does not ask for location, contacts, storage, the camera or the microphone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous versions remain visible in the public repository this page is published from.

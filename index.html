VIT

Programmes from Kringvarp Føroya, on Google TV and on your telephone.

VIT presents VIT Sjón and VIT Ljóð — Kringvarp Føroya's television and radio programmes — as one catalogue you can browse with a remote or a thumb. Programmes are played directly from KVF's own servers; nothing is copied or stored.

What it does

  • Series and films for television, kept apart from radio.
  • Continues where you stopped, and shows the position it would resume from.
  • Plays the next episode when one finishes.
  • Remembers nothing about you, and shows no advertising.
